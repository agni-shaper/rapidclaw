# Team holidays

Days the whole team is off (national / company-wide holidays in IST). Routines that nudge teammates or post team-facing reports should skip these days entirely — `is_working_day` in `accountability/routines/_lib.sh` reads this file.

Format: `<YYYY-MM-DD> · short name` (one date per line, IST). For multi-day breaks, add one line per day.

Add a line under *Upcoming* when a new holiday is known. Move expired entries to *Past* when convenient; no auto-prune.
