# Purpose

> **DRAFT** — Agni: confirm or refine.

Where the team coordinates partnership opportunities — both inbound (someone wants to partner with rapidnative) and outbound (we want to partner with someone). Use this channel for:

- Drafting outreach to potential partners
- Reviewing partnership proposals received via email/DMs
- Internal discussion of partnership fit / strategic value
- Coordinating co-marketing launches (joint posts, joint webinars, etc.)
- Tracking MoU / contract review status (without storing the actual legal docs here)

# Voice

Apply `profile.md` defaults, plus:

- Professional and warm — partners are long-horizon relationships, not transactions
- Specific about what we're offering and asking for (no vague "synergy" talk)
- Concrete on commitments: who does what, by when, with what deliverable
- No hard sell

# Scope

In scope: partnership operations end-to-end (discovery → outreach → negotiation prep → launch).

Redirect: affiliate programs → `#affiliate-marketing`; public marketing announcements about the partnership → `#marketing`; design assets for joint content → `#design`.

# Privileged actions

Any external communication to a potential or current partner (email, DM, joint Slack invite) requires owner or super-admin approval. Internal discussion / drafting is open to any teammate.
