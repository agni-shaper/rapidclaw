## Product positioning (provisional)

- **What it is:** Mobile-app deploy service. Takes the pain out of fastlane / Expo EAS / TestFlight / Play Console for indie devs and small teams.
- **Who it's for:** Indie devs shipping mobile apps who hit the iOS/Android publishing wall. App owners who don't want to learn Apple's certificate jungle.
- **Wedge:** "Push to git, ship to App Store."

## Topic pillars (provisional)

1. **Mobile-app deploy pain** — fastlane, Expo EAS, TestFlight, Play Console gotchas. Real stories of "I spent 3 days on this".
2. **Release engineering for indie devs** — versioning, code-signing, beta channels, hotfix flows.
3. **(More pending repo + brand audit.)**

## Voice for LetsDeployIt drafts (provisional)

- Empathetic to the pain. "We've all been there with code signing." Builder solidarity.
- Specifics: error messages, certificate names, the exact step that failed.
- Time-to-resolution framing. Money-flow framing for indie devs (every deploy day not shipped = no revenue).
- Cross-brand defaults from `profile.md` still apply.

## Platforms (provisional)

1. **r/reactnative**, **r/iOSProgramming**, **r/androiddev** — directly painful audiences.
2. **HackerNews** — Show HN at major releases.
3. **X (Twitter)** — RN + mobile dev community.
4. **dev.to / Hashnode** — long-form "how I fixed X" tutorials with LetsDeployIt as the bottom-of-funnel.
5. **Expo Discord / Slack** — where the EAS pain shows up live.

(Cross-promote with RapidNative on RN-specific channels — natural pairing since LetsDeployIt is what you use AFTER RapidNative.)

## SEO / content priorities

- Long-tail around "fastlane <error message>", "Expo EAS <error>", "TestFlight stuck on processing", "Play Console signing".
- Comparison content (LetsDeployIt vs Expo EAS, LetsDeployIt vs Codemagic, LetsDeployIt vs Bitrise).
- Tutorial funnel: "How to deploy an Expo app in 2026" → CTA for LetsDeployIt.
